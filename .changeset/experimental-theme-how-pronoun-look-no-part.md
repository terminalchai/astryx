[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a bare look taken away by a pronoun after a change that starts with make, set, use, give, add, change or turn, joined before it by "and", "but" or "then" ("make the buttons blue and the border is ugly, hide it"), is never that change's part: nothing is written for it, and the answer asks which part. Other forms (another verb, a comma, a new line, "!", a question before the ask, or a look said with other words) keep the reading they have.
