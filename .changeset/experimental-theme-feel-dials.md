[experimental] The theme editor has two feel dials, `press` and `hover` (darker, lighter, stronger, none, or a percent from 1% to 60%). Each sets one wash for the whole theme that every part draws over its own fill while pressed or hovered, so the look follows each part's colors in every variant and mode. A part whose pressed or hover background the theme sets keeps the base wash there. `how` reads "darker on press" and "8% tint on hover" as the dial.
